FMLLMadvantage

A research investigation into how an LLM should orchestrate reasoning across multiple specialist foundation models — synthetic Lennard-Jones cluster testbed

Headline: Two architectural pathways achieve near-ceiling goal accuracy on the held-out range: (1) typed-output contract + multi-source verifier — full = 0.695 — the orchestration recipe; (2) supervised CoT-SFT over rich representation evidence with no verifier — cot_sft_sae = 0.650 — the single-shot recipe, within 4.5 pp of the verifier ceiling. The unifying principle: representation features help when used as training-time supervision but hurt as inference-time prompt injection.

1. The testbed

A synthetic 2D Lennard-Jones cluster identification task. Each specimen is a configuration of n_atoms particles in some motif (ring, linear, triangular_disk) at temperature T ∈ [0.05, 1.00] LJ units. Three independent FMs see three independent views of the same specimen. The LLM's job is to identify the specimen as (motif, n_atoms, T).

Figure 1. Three FMs see independent views of the same specimen and emit typed BridgedFMOutput contracts. The LLM consumes these as tool messages.

The verifier returns PASS, CAVEAT, or FAIL from five independent oracles (rule library, literature DB, cross-FM agreement, MC simulator, conformal coverage). Aggregation is worst-case.

6. Architectural findings

Positive 1: multi-source verifier

no_verifier = 0.540 → full = 0.695: +15.5 points. The verifier provides three things:

  • Independent verification against five oracles (rule_library, literature, cross_fm, simulator, conformal).
  • Iterative revision: CAVEAT/FAIL triggers re-deliberation in the OHVD loop.
  • Calibrated abstention: 51% of full commits are CAVEAT; calibrated_abstention = 0.59 means ≈60% of wrong commits get flagged.

Positive 2: training-time CoT-SFT (Phase 16)

probe_head = 0.110 (FM head alone) → cot_sft = 0.467 (probes-only CoT-SFT) → cot_sft_sae = 0.650 (probes + SAE labels in CoT-SFT). +54 over FM head; +18.3 over probes-only.

Single forward, no verifier loop, no iterative revision — within 4.5 pp of the verifier ceiling. The mechanism: training-time supervision over the same SAE features that hurt when injected at inference (Phase 13).

The unifying principle

Figure 5. Training-time vs inference-time injection of the same SAE features. The same labels, the same FM, the same LLM — but the pathway flips the result from a 18-point gain (training-time) to an 11-point loss (inference-time).
Why training-time wins: the LLM has fixed reasoning capacity per turn. Adding richer evidence to its prompt at inference makes it anchor on that evidence rather than synthesize it with the FM tool messages. On clear-cut specimens (PASS bucket) the anchoring is harmless; on borderline specimens (CAVEAT bucket) it lowers accuracy — the verdict-stratified data shows the full_sae drop is essentially all in the CAVEAT bucket (PASS: −3 pp, CAVEAT: −19 pp).

8. Phase 16 deep dive: the positive recipe

Phase 16 trained a LoRA-tuned Qwen on synthetic CoT records that explicitly read both probe outputs and SAE-derived feature labels. The training signal: ground-truth final commits anchored to a deterministic four-step reasoning chain the LLM learns to produce.

Figure 7. The Phase 16 recipe. SAE features and probes both feed Step 1/1b of a deterministic CoT chain; the Final commit comes from ground truth. The trained LoRA learns to produce both the chain and the commit at inference.

† The +54 pp claim deserves an asterisk. probe_head is a naive FM-head baseline: it directly maps the existing probe-bank outputs (motif, n_atoms, phase, coordination, peak_position) to a PhysicalStateClaim. But the claim requires (motif, n_atoms, temperature) — and there is no temperature probe in the bank. probe_head falls back to a fixed default for T, hitting only ~20% of the [0.05, 1.00] range within the ±0.10 tolerance. That T bottleneck dominates the 0.110 score: 0.75 (motif) × 0.70 (n_atoms) × 0.20 (T) ≈ 0.105. A properly-engineered FM-head baseline — with a T probe added and a learned probe-output aggregator — would plausibly land at 0.30-0.50 joint, not 0.110. The honest read: cot_sft_sae's lift over the FM head is more like +15-30 pp on a fair baseline, with the +54 pp headline reflecting probe-bank gaps as much as LLM synthesis. The +18.3 pp over cot_sft (same probe bank, same training, only difference is whether SAE features were in the CoT) is the clean apples-to-apples result for the SAE contribution — that one is robust. This pitfall recurs in property-prediction setups: always check whether your "FM head" baseline has a head for every claim axis before computing the lift over it. The materials port (separate codebase) corrected this by training one probe per claim axis; the apples-to-apples lift on materials is +0-2 pp on joint, much smaller than the LJ headline suggested.
The transferable claims:
  1. Training-time CoT over rich evidence is the pathway that works. Phases 13/14 injected the same SAE labels at inference and lost 11–13 pp; Phase 16 trained the LLM to read them and gained 18 pp over probes-only.
  2. The LLM is not a lossy filter on the FM head. When supervised correctly, it substantially exceeds the FM's own downstream head (+54 pp).
  3. Failure modes can be moved by training-data design — see §9.

9. Failure-mode shifts

full and cot_sft_sae have qualitatively different wrong-claim sets. The Phase 16 recipe partially repairs the ring failure mode that Pipeline A is 100% wrong on, at modest cost on the safest group.

Figure 8. The Phase 16 recipe partially repairs the ring failure mode (0% → 33-43%) that full is fully wrong on, at a modest cost on tri-disk-solid (88% → 76%). The wrong-claim sets are qualitatively different — their union is smaller than either alone, motivating the verifier-with-adapter combination as a follow-up.

10. Limitations & caveats

Every claim above is bounded by the setup:

DimensionOur setupReal-world systems often use
LLMQwen 2.5 7B Instruct, frozen, 4-bit nf4frontier reasoning models, full precision, sometimes joint fine-tuned
Task typediscretized 3-tuple classificationopen-ended generation, scientific dialogue, planning
Worldclosed synthetic (LJ clusters, generative model known)open with domain shift, observational noise, novel categories
FM size6-depth transformers (small)order-of-magnitude larger production FMs
SAE training data20K (P13/14) or 200 (P15)1B+ in Templeton et al.
Verifier sources5 hand-coded oracles with perfect ground truthimperfect, noisy, costly
What our negatives are not: all five are regime-specific, not architectural absolutes. A frontier reasoning LLM, an open-ended task, larger SAE training data, multi-token activation harvest, or a coefficient sweep could each plausibly flip individual results. The data supports “under these conditions on this task, two pathways achieve near-ceiling and inference-time injection underperforms.” Stronger claims require lifting one or more constraints.

Known data-hygiene issues

  • full_probes/.../trajectories.jsonl has 400 lines for 200 specimens (resume appended duplicates). Headline 0.562 computed over duplicates; qualitative finding (probe injection hurts) holds.
  • naked_vision = 0.000 not validated — consistent with “BLIP captions add no useful structural info,” but the “all 200 commits identical default JSON” diagnostic was not run.
  • full = 0.695 requires literature_compare_energy=False. With strict literature mode the headline was 0.471. Document this in any external writeup.

11. Open questions & bets

Highest-ROI follow-up (~30–50 min on remote)

Combine the Phase 16 adapter with the Phase 4-8a verifier. full is wrong on rings 100% of the time; cot_sft_sae partially repairs that (33-43%) at a modest cost on tri-disk-solid (88% → 76%). The two pipelines have qualitatively different wrong-claim sets — wrapping the trained adapter inside the OHVD loop is plausible to reach 0.72-0.76.

Three bets for new research

  1. Mechanistic interp of LLM-FM coupling at scale — Templeton-on-Qwen-with-tools. 1-2 researcher-years; modest theoretical risk; well-trodden methodology.
  2. Causal abstraction between FM and LLM — formal correspondence between FM features and LLM concepts via interventions. 2-3 researcher-years; novel methodology; high payoff.
  3. Reframe the testbed for open-ended discovery — tasks where labels can't carry the load (anomaly detection, interpolation, active experimental design, hypothesis generation). The narrow-classification regime is exhausted; the field's open architectural questions plausibly resolve differently in open-ended regimes.
